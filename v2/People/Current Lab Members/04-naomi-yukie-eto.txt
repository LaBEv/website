Nome: Naomi Yukie Eto
Vínculo: PhD student
Formação: Undergraduate studies in Biological Sciences at FFCLRP-USP · PhD studies in Genetics at Ribeirão Preto Medical School, University of São Paulo (FMRP-USP)
Pesquisa: Evolutionary genetics, genetic diversity, gene flow and mitochondrial–nuclear interactions in cactus-associated Drosophila.
Bio: She is a PhD student in Genetics at FMRP-USP and conducts her doctoral research at LaBEv. Her undergraduate research with Maura Helena Manfrin examined introgression and genetic diversity in Drosophila gouveai. She is the first author of a 2025 study comparing genetic diversity and introgression between D. gouveai and D. antonietae in Analândia, São Paulo.
