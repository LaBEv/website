Nome: Emeline Boni Campanini
Vínculo: Former PhD student
Formação: M.Sc. (2011) and Ph.D. (2016), Graduate Program in Evolutionary Genetics and Molecular Biology, Federal University of São Carlos
Pesquisa: Evolutionary genetics, genomics, transcriptomics and the evolution of odorant-binding proteins in fruit flies.
Bio: Her master's research examined genetic diversity in Brazilian populations of the cattle egret. During her FAPESP-funded PhD with Reinaldo de Brito, she compared the evolution and expression of odorant-binding protein genes in Anastrepha fraterculus and Anastrepha obliqua. She began CAPES-supported postdoctoral research at the Federal University of São Carlos in 2018.
