Nome: Emanuele Cristina Gustani
Vínculo: Former PhD student
Formação: M.Sc. in Evolutionary Biology, Midwestern Paraná State University and State University of Ponta Grossa (2013) · Ph.D. in Evolutionary Genetics and Molecular Biology, Federal University of São Carlos (2018)
Pesquisa: Quantitative evolutionary genetics, morphological evolution and phylogeography.
Bio: Her master's research explored the evolutionary history and population structure of two Drosophila species from the Atlantic Forest. At LaBEv, she studied the evolution of correlated traits in Anastrepha fruit flies, including how host plants affect their morphology. During her PhD, she worked with Jason B. Wolf at the University of Bath on the quantitative genetics of stalk-eyed flies (Teleopsis dalmanni). Her later research has applied phylogeography to respiratory virus circulation.
