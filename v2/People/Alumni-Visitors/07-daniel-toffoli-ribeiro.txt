Nome: Daniel Toffoli Ribeiro
Vínculo: Former PhD student
Formação: B.Sc. in Biological Sciences, Federal University of Uberlândia · M.Sc. in Biological Sciences, National Institute for Amazonian Research and Federal University of Amazonas (2006) · Ph.D. in Evolutionary Genetics and Molecular Biology, Federal University of São Carlos (2013)
Pesquisa: Molecular systematics, phylogeography and population genetics of Neotropical freshwater fishes, particularly stingrays of the family Potamotrygonidae.
Bio: His master's research examined the evolutionary history of Potamotrygon stingrays in the Amazon Basin. At LaBEv, he investigated the diversification and hybridization of South American freshwater stingrays, relating their evolutionary history to historical changes in river drainage.
